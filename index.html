Somo

I grew up in a small city in the middle of nowhere in the Middle East. I wanted to emigrate, so I had no choice but to start working. The internet, markets and crypto gave me the freedom, and the means, to get what I wanted.

Now I work at a positioning advisory firm. AI made building cheap and words free, so what sets a company apart now is what the market believes about it, and whether it can prove it. We help frontier tech companies decide what they want to be known for, then build the proof behind it.

I started my career trying to beat Crunchbase in the digital asset space with my first startup, Longlist. That’s where I learned how venture works, and it led me to Sharding Capital, a VC firm based in the UAE, where I worked as an analyst. Later, with three other founders, I started a team that built several DeFi products, where I owned growth.
