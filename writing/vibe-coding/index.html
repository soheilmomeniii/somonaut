Now you can just build the thing. Vibe coding killed the gap between "I think this should exist" and "here, try it."

The venture world feels this first. The best VCs won't be the ones with the sharpest memos. They'll be the ones shipping. A fund that builds its own tools says more about its edge than any letter to LPs. The unlock is when the build itself is the content. Not the behind-the-scenes. The thing.

Think about what you actually go back to. You might like an article, maybe even bookmark it. You'll never open it again. But a tool that's useful?

An article earns a moment of attention. A tool earns a habit.

That's the difference.

The people shaping how others think won't be the best writers. They'll be the best builders. Not because writing died. Because building became writing.
